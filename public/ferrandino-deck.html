03Market Context

Why the market is changing.

Structural shifts in electricity demand and grid capacity are creating durable tailwinds.

AI driving electricity demand

Data centers and compute growth are accelerating load faster than the grid can respond.

Grid congestion

Interconnection queues and constrained transmission are slowing new capacity.

Utility constraints

Utilities increasingly rely on distributed and flexible resources to meet demand.

Corporate sustainability

Commercial owners and tenants want lower-carbon, resilient energy solutions.

Energy resilience

Businesses want backup power and protection from outages.

Aging electric infrastructure

Much of the grid was built decades ago and needs modernization.
