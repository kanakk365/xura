📊 Where the capital flows

  • Tech / SaaS — highest capital concentration (the "operating system" race)

  • Facilities — >$2.5B (2024–26) but flagged over-capitalized
  • Academies · media · insurance/safety — emerging tiers

⚠️ The counter-currents

  • "Let Kids Play Act" (May 2026) — would brand PE "vulture investors," ban roll-ups & 150-mi tournament bans, force divestiture (treble damages)
  • Pay-to-play — ~33% of families take on debt; income gap widened 13.6→20.2 pts
  • Demographics — births down ~15% → ~30% cohort shrink/decade; baseball −19% (2019–24)
  • Execution — "lack of scaled assets is the real constraint, not conviction"
08 — Where DKP Fits

A fragmented market is an aggregator's opportunity.

Capital is consolidating operators and facilities — but the family-facing layer (discovery, access, the connective tissue between kids and programs) remains wide open. That's the seam a destination platform plays in.

🧩 Fragmentation = opening

U.S. youth soccer alone is a maze of overlapping leagues (ECNL, MLS NEXT, USYS, AYSO). No single family-facing front door exists.

💸 Spend is flowing

34M parents pay into a $40B market growing 8–10%/yr, with per-child spend up 46% since 2019. Demand is structural, not cyclical.

🏟️ Tailwinds everywhere

$1B+ complexes, flag football at LA28, and record PE inflows all expand the top of the funnel for a discovery/marketplace layer.
