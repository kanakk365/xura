04 — Market Area Analysis

A market that spends above the national average on sport.

The market for an amateur sports and recreation facility is the region where its regular users live. HVS defined it as a 1-hour drive from the venue. Tournaments draw from further: 3 hours for smaller events and up to 5 hours for larger, regional ones.

1-hour drive · regular users 3-hour · smaller tournaments 5-hour · regional events

What the drive-time market shows

  • High household formation, high household income and a strong propensity to spend on sport give the strongest possible support for youth and adult participation sports.
  • Demand is strongest within a 60-minute drive; the longer drive-time markets strongly support regional tournaments.
  • Excellent airport access will support national tournaments.
  • The area around the development spends above the national average on fees for participant sports.

Market Area Analysis, continued

Wealth & growth

  • On the W&P Wealth Index (national average = 100), Nassau and Suffolk Counties are wealthier than the New York metro area, the tri-state area and New York State.
  • HVS's review of economic and demographic forecasts supports modest growth in every Nassau and Suffolk metric: a stable, growing local market.
  • More residents in the one-hour drive time hold white-collar jobs than the national average, and the markets are diversified across industries.
  • Seven of Suffolk County's top ten employers are in health care (Northwell Health, Catholic Health Service and others), social assistance, or state and local government.

Spending & participation

  • Every drive-time market spends more per household on sports and recreation than the national average.
  • Residents within the one-hour drive spend significantly more per household on recreational sports fees and lessons than the broader drive-time markets.
  • In all drive times, household participation in baseball, basketball, soccer, softball and volleyball is higher than the national average.

How the ranges were set

  • Aluminum grandstands run $100–$500 per seat; larger grandstands with VIP seating run $1,000+ per seat (GT Grandstands). This estimate carries standard seats at $300–$500 and premium seating at $1,000–$1,500.
  • Suites are carried at $1,500–$3,000 per guest position and the safe-standing deck at $150–$300 per place, extending the same benchmark.
  • That works out to $672–$1,150 per spectator, against $10,000+ per seat for a purpose-built soccer stadium.
Not in the budget: field lighting, restroom and concession buildings, utilities, scoreboard or video board, and parking. The stadium wraps Field 1, one of the complex's turf fields, so no new playing surface is priced.
