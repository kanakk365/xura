02Market Context

Why the market is changing.

Structural shifts in electricity demand and grid capacity are creating durable tailwinds.

AI driving electricity demand

Data centers and compute growth are accelerating load faster than the grid can respond.

Grid congestion

Interconnection queues and constrained transmission are slowing new capacity.

Utility constraints

Utilities increasingly rely on distributed and flexible resources to meet demand.

Corporate sustainability

Commercial owners and tenants want lower-carbon, resilient energy solutions.

Energy resilience

Businesses want backup power and protection from outages.

Aging electric infrastructure

Much of the grid was built decades ago and needs modernization.

18Ken Garnett
Ken Garnett
Managing Director, Head of Special Situations

Ken is the Head of Intrepid’s Special Situations practice. He has nearly 30 years of experience involving stressed, distressed, and complex M&A and financing transactions. He has led transactions in a variety of industries including retail, branded and non-branded consumer products, manufacturing, metals and mining, oil & gas, specialty finance, transportation, technology and software.

Before joining Intrepid, Ken was a Managing Director in Cowen & Company’s Special Situations Group and served as Managing Director in Conway MacKenzie’s distressed private funds practice. Prior to that, Ken founded and was partner at Renewal Capital, a private equity fund that focused on special situation investments across the capital structure of middle market companies. Additionally, Ken was a Partner at Gordian Group, a restructuring boutique investment bank in New York, and worked at Deloitte Consulting in its strategic operations/turnaround consulting practice.

Ken is a Certified Public Accountant (inactive) and an Expert Witness in U.S. Bankruptcy Courts and other venues.

He received his MBA from New York University, where he was named a Stern Scholar, and a BA in Economics from Davidson College. He also studied at the London School of Economics.
