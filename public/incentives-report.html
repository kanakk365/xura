01 — Federal

Federal incentives — credits, grants & loans.

Federal tax credits, grants and loan programs spanning EV & charging, clean generation, battery storage, fuel cells, linear-generator manufacturing, green farming and broad decarbonization. Program names link to the IRS / DOE / USDA / DOT source; the agency column links to the administering body.

Time-critical — OBBBA (P.L. 119-21) reshaped the federal stack in 2025. Consumer EV credits (30D / 25E / 45W) ended Sept 30, 2025; the 30C EV-charging credit terminates for property placed in service after June 30, 2026; 179D and 45L end for construction/homes after June 30, 2026; wind/solar 48E/45Y phase out from 2027. 02 — SBA Loans

SBA financing — 504 & 7(a).

Small Business Administration lending that finances energy, storage and EV-charging projects — including the 504 program's "green / energy public policy" track, which lifts the cap for projects that cut energy use ≥10% or self-generate ≥15% from renewables.
